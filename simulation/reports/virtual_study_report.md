> result and nothing in it may be cited as a finding about plants.** The hidden data-generating model, its planted

## 8. RQ3 rehearsal - mechanistic vs ML vs hybrid forecast of harvest dry mass (leave-one-run-out, unit level)

Dry mass at the forecast day is taken from vision through an allometric fit on destructive samples (dw = 0.0002 x area^1.64).

| model | MAPE % @ day 7 | MAPE % @ day 14 | MAPE % @ day 21 |
|---|---|---|---|
| hybrid | 8.87 | 9.6 | 7.95 |
| mechanistic | 19.1 | 9.27 | 26.7 |
| ml_gbm | 15.8 | 9.8 | 8.66 |
| ml_ridge | 12.6 | 10.4 | 11.7 |

| comparison | diff | ci_low | ci_high | n_clusters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hybrid - mechanistic (mean |error|, g) | -0.584 | -0.699 | -0.458 | 16 |
| hybrid - ml_gbm (mean |error|, g) | -0.174 | -0.317 | -0.0574 | 16 |

Negative difference = hybrid better. The simulator's truth is the same model family as the mechanistic twin with shifted parameters, so this comparison is favourable to the physics-based models and cannot be read as evidence for RQ3.

## 9. Twin calibration (parameter recovery)

Fitted on the 4 control pod-runs (serial + final harvest dry mass and leaf area); log-RMSE 0.226:

| parameter | twin_default | estimate | se | simulator_truth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| c_eps | 1.7e-05 | 1.199e-05 | 1.277e-06 | 1.53e-05 |
| c_gr_max | 5e-06 | 1.104e-05 | 4.355e-06 | 5.6e-06 |
| sla_obs | 0.035 | 0.03166 | 0.002661 | 0.032 |

The estimates should move from the twin defaults towards the simulator's hidden values; unit and run random effects (which the fit absorbs into the parameters) prevent exact recovery. `c_eps` and `c_gr_max` are partly confounded.
