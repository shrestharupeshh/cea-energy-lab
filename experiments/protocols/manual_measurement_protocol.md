# Manual measurement protocol (ground truth)

Purpose: produce reference measurements against which automated estimates are judged. A reference
method has its own error; that error is measured (section 5) and reported alongside the
automated-method error.

## 1. Instruments

| Quantity | Instrument | Resolution | Check before each session |
|----------|-----------|-----------|---------------------------|
| Fresh / dry mass | Analytical balance, 0.01 g (≥ 600 g capacity) | 0.01 g | 100 g and 200 g check weights within ± 0.02 g |
| Diameter, height | 300 mm steel rule; digital caliper for < 150 mm | 1 mm / 0.01 mm | zero |
| Leaf area (destructive) | Flatbed scanner 300 dpi + `plantpulse.computer_vision.scanner_area` | 0.007 mm² px⁻¹ | scan the 100 cm² reference card; accept within ± 1 % |
| Drying | Forced-air oven 70 °C | - | thermometer check |
| Leaf greenness (optional) | SPAD-502 or equivalent | 0.1 unit | manufacturer check plate |

## 2. Non-destructive session (twice weekly, all plants)

Order of pods and of plants within a pod is randomized per session (sheet generated by
`plantpulse.stats.design.measurement_order`). Keep door-open time ≤ 4 min per pod; the door switch
logs it.

1. Trigger an image set immediately before opening the door (`session_pre`).
2. Leaf count: leaves > 10 mm long, excluding cotyledons.
3. Diameter: maximum horizontal extent, and the extent perpendicular to it; record both.
4. Height: lid surface to highest point of the undisturbed canopy.
5. Visual scores (0-4 scales defined on the data sheet): tipburn, chlorosis, wilting, necrosis.
   For blinded scoring use the image set, displayed by the dashboard with pod codes hidden.
6. SPAD: youngest fully expanded leaf, 3 readings, mean (optional).
7. Enter values in the dashboard form (double entry for 10 % of plants, automatically compared).

## 3. Destructive harvest

1. Image set immediately before harvest.
2. Cut shoot at the substrate surface. Weigh shoot fresh mass within 60 s (transpirational loss).
3. Count leaves (> 10 mm). Detach leaves, lay flat without overlap on the scanner with the
   reference card; scan; file name from `plantpulse.data.naming.scan_name`.
4. Roots: rinse, blot 30 s, weigh (fresh), bag.
5. Dry shoot and root in labelled paper bags at 70 °C to constant mass (≥ 72 h; re-weigh a 10 %
   subsample after a further 24 h, accept change < 1 %). Weigh within 2 min of leaving the oven or
   from a desiccator.
6. Record tipburn leaf count.

## 4. Data integrity

- Balance values are typed twice by two people or captured over the balance's serial port.
- Bag labels carry plant ID as QR + text. Labels are printed from the database, never handwritten.
- Any deviation (dropped leaf, delayed weighing) is entered as an `intervention` record.

## 5. Reference-method repeatability (do once per operator, and once per study)

- Diameter, height, leaf count: two operators measure the same 12 plants independently, twice
  each. Compute repeatability and reproducibility SD (gauge R&R, ANOVA method).
- Scanner leaf area: scan paper shapes of known area (5 sizes, 10-400 cm²) at three placements.
- Balance: 10 repeat weighings of one fresh shoot in a closed container.

The reference repeatability SD is the floor for any automated-vs-manual comparison: an automated
method cannot be shown to be more accurate than the reference is repeatable.

## 6. Comparison of automated and manual values

Computed by `plantpulse.computer_vision.evaluation`: bias, 95 % limits of agreement, MAE, RMSE,
MAPE, Lin's CCC, regression of difference on mean (proportional bias), and R² for information
only. A high R² with a large bias or wide limits of agreement is a failed validation.
